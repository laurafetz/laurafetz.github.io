Related simulation research tests how competing models can account for similar patterns. It encourages researchers to consider network explanations alongside factor models. The bigger question: when does a well-fitting model actually help explain the process behind the data?

I used multilevel models to analyse student learning outcomes in large educational datasets.

Explore the direction

Prowise’s wider research explores how to measure changing skills and individual differences in adaptive learning systems. That could help us distinguish learning progress from differences in tasks and practice, and design more responsive support.

Family safety

I analysed single-case data on family-based interventions addressing child abuse and co-authored a research manuscript.

Explore the direction
